# Evaluation Opportunities: High-Risk & System Gaps

## 1. High-Risk AI Usage Scenarios
These scenarios represent the "Red Zone" where the Evaluation Mode is most critical:
- **Tax & Financial Reviews**: Subhojyoti (Tax Analyst) uses AI for return reviews. A single error here is catastrophic.
- **Academic Submissions**: Riya and Soumya use AI for assignments. Inaccuracies lead to grade loss and academic integrity issues.
- **Coding & Technical Architecture**: Navarup and Shwetlana use AI for coding. "Subtle errors" in logic can ruin entire systems.
- **Medical/Legal/Factual Research**: Sanchita highlights these as "High-Stakes" where data volatility is high.

## 2. Identified System Gaps
Current AI interfaces fail users in the following ways:
- **Lack of "Source Existence" checks**: They provide links, but don't verify if they work.
- **No "Certainty" Visualization**: Users can't tell which part of a paragraph the AI is "unsure" about.
- **Persistent Persuasiveness**: The tone is always confident, even when wrong.
- **No Human Sign-off**: No built-in way to track what has been verified by a human.

## 3. Intervention Opportunities
- **The "Pause" Intervention**: Forcing a delay on high-risk topics to encourage human thought.
- **The "Neutral" Filter**: Re-rendering AI prose into dry, clinical facts.
- **The "Logic Tree"**: Exposing the Chain-of-Thought (CoT) steps for logical verification.
- **Collaborative Verification**: Allowing multiple users to annotate and sign off on a shared AI output.
