# Insights Summary: Survey Findings

## 1. The "Fluency vs. Accuracy" Trap
Nearly **50% of users** admit to skipping reviews because the AI output sounds "convincing." This confirms that professional formatting and a confident tone are the primary enemies of critical evaluation.

## 2. Intent-Based Skepticism
Users already have a mental "risk map." They naturally verify Research and Career tasks more than personal productivity. **Evaluation Mode** should leverage this by automatically increasing friction when it detects these high-risk intents.

## 3. Feature Gap: Beyond Citations
While "Source Citations" are often discussed, users prioritized **"Fact-checking Support"** and **"Human Review Suggestions"** much higher. They don't just want the AI to show its work; they want the system to actively flag *where* a human must intervene.

## 4. Time is the Enemy
Verification is seen as a "chore" that consumes the time AI saved. To be successful, Evaluation Mode must make verification **seamless and integrated**, not an external multi-step process.

## 5. Trust is Earned, Not Given
With over **80% of users** expressing some form of skepticism, the application should not try to "convince" users to trust AI. Instead, it should position itself as a **verification tool** that helps users reach their own conclusions.
